Billing Management Module

Team Member: Member 3

Objective

To manage billing, invoices, taxes and discounts in the Subscription Management System.

Main Features

1. Invoice Generation

- Generate invoices from active subscriptions.
- Automatically retrieve customer and product details.
- Calculate invoice totals.
- Prevent duplicate billing.

2. Invoice Management

- Draft, Confirmed and Paid statuses.
- Confirm, cancel, send and print invoices.

3. Tax Management

- Calculate taxes automatically.
- Use administrator-configured tax rules.
- Display tax amounts and final totals.

4. Discount Management

- Fixed and percentage discounts.
- Minimum purchase and quantity conditions.
- Start and end dates.
- Usage limits.
- Product-specific or subscription-wide discounts.
- Automatically apply eligible discounts to invoices.

Integration

- Receive customer details from Member 1.
- Receive subscription and product details from Member 2.
- Share invoice information with Member 4 for payment tracking.

Development Status

Planning and preparation.
